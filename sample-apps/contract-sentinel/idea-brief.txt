Build an MCP application called Contract Sentinel.

It manages business contracts and must behave as an autonomous
agent, not just a reactive tool-caller. Structure it like this:

1. A tool set_company_profile(industry, companySize, jurisdiction,
   riskTolerance) that stores company context for the session, used
   by every other tool.

2. A tool ingest_contract(contractText) that stores a contract with
   a unique id, extracts its key clauses, deadline date, and
   obligations into structured data, and adds it to a tracked
   contract list.

3. A resource contracts://portfolio that exposes the full list of
   tracked contracts with their current status, score, and
   classification (safe or danger).

4. An MCP Task called run_sentinel_cycle that runs the actual agent
   loop, NOT triggered by a single user question:
   - perceive: read every tracked contract from the portfolio
   - decide: for each one, score its risk (always attach the
     specific clause text that drove the score, plus a disclaimer
     that this is not legal advice), and decide if it needs action
     (deadline is close, risk score is high, or nothing has happened
     on it yet)
   - act: for contracts needing action, automatically update their
     status to "needs_attention" and record what action is
     recommended (renew as-is / renegotiate with specific talking
     points / let lapse)
   This task should be runnable on demand but designed to represent
   something that would run on a schedule in production.

5. A widget contract-board showing two columns, Safe and Danger,
   sourced from the portfolio resource, where each card shows the
   risk score and clicking it reveals the exact clause text that
   caused the classification.

Keep the reasoning behind every score fully visible in the output —
never return a bare score with no explanation.
