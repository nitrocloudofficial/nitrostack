# ⚡ ForgeMind AI — Autonomous AI Factory Operations Copilot
**Track:** Track 02 — Manufacturing & Industry 4.0  
**Team:** Team Yukti — Amrita University Coimbatore  

## Overview
ForgeMind AI is an autonomous 7-agent predictive maintenance copilot for Industry 4.0. It continuously monitors factory IoT streams, predicts machine failure risks (RUL ~4h), performs Explainable AI root-cause analysis, simulates production rerouting to save ₹12 Lakhs/event, and auto-dispatches emergency maintenance tickets.
