# Standard Operating Procedure: Hybrid Work Security Policy
**Document ID**: SOP-005
**Author**: Evelyn Reed (Head of People & Culture)
**Date**: 2020-10-15
**Version**: 2.0 (Active)

## Scope
This policy regulates employee network access behaviors when working from home.

## Operational Standards
All remote workers must login through a VPN gateway. Compliance audit requirements dictate logging session data under the guidance of Deborah.

## Rejected Alternatives
* **Direct external access**: Rejected as a high risk for data leakage.

## Consequences
Failure to utilize verified VPN tunnels will result in immediate credential suspension.
