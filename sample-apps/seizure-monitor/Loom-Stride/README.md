# ✨ What is LOOM&STRIDE?

**LOOM** is an intelligent footwear compatibility platform that helps people discover shoes that truly fit—not just by size, but by **foot geometry, biomechanics, lifestyle, and comfort requirements**.

Most footwear recommendations stop at:

> **"What's your shoe size?"**

LOOM asks the more important question:

> **"Which shoe is actually built for your feet?"**

Using computer vision, explainable AI, functional assessments, and transparent decision-making, LOOM analyzes your feet and recommends shoes based on compatibility rather than guesswork.

---

# 🚀 What is STRIDE?

**STRIDE** is the recommendation engine powering LOOM & STRIDE.

It combines:

- 📷 Computer Vision
- 🧠 Explainable AI
- 📊 Multi-Criteria Decision Making
- 👣 Foot Biomechanics
- ⚙️ Agentic AI
- 🔗 NitroStack MCP

to generate recommendations that are **transparent**, **evidence-informed**, and **fully explainable**.

---

# 🌍 The Problem

Millions of people buy shoes online every day.

Yet returns remain extremely high because footwear recommendations are based almost entirely on **shoe size**.

The reality is:

- Two people with the same size can have completely different feet.
- Foot width varies.
- Toe shapes vary.
- Walking patterns differ.
- Medical conditions affect footwear needs.
- Every brand fits differently.

Size alone isn't enough.
