### What It Does
```

         ↓
Agent extracts courses and calculates current CGPA
         ↓
Student sets target CGPA and available study time
         ↓
Agent calculates study load per course
         ↓
Agent generates detailed study schedule
         ↓
Student specifies constraints (work, days off, pace)
         ↓
Agent adjusts schedule accordingly
         ↓
Student exports schedule to calendar
         ↓
Student follows schedule and achieves target CGPA! 🎉
```
