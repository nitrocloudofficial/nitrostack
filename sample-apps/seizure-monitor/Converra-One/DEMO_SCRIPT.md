# Converra One - 5-Minute Hackathon Demo Script

> **Goal**: Deliver a flawless, high-impact 5-minute live demonstration showcasing Converra One to hackathon judges.

---

## ⏱️ Timed Presentation Flow (Total: 5:00)

### 0:00 - 0:45 | 1. Problem & Vision
- **Speaker**: "In modern engineering and enterprise environments, context fragmentation is rampant. Communication happens across Gmail, Slack, GitHub, Discord, Notion, and Google Calendar. Workers spend up to 3 hours a day manually toggling apps, missing crucial deadlines, and drowning in unread noise."
- **Action**: Open Converra One Dashboard. Point out the dark-mode glassmorphism studio theme.
- **Key Takeaway**: "Converra One brings every conversation into a single intelligent workspace powered by Model Context Protocol (MCP) and multi-agent AI."

---

### 0:45 - 1:30 | 2. Morning Briefing & Unified Inbox
- **Speaker**: "Instead of sifting through 100 emails, our SummaryAgent and PriorityAgent automatically synthesize an Executive Morning Briefing and score every message's urgency from 0.00 to 1.00."
- **Action**: Click on an urgent email from Dr. Evelyn Vance regarding CS340 architecture and Raft consensus.
- **Key Takeaway**: "Cross-platform inbox unification with real-time priority scoring."

---

### 1:30 - 2:15 | 3. Multi-Tone Smart Reply & Action Item Extraction
- **Speaker**: "Notice how our TaskAgent automatically extracts deliverables like 'Review Raft parameters before 3 PM', while ReplyAgent drafts context-aware responses across 5 distinct tones."
- **Action**: Click 'Generate Reply' -> Select 'Professional'. Click 'Insert Reply'.
- **Key Takeaway**: "Instant, context-aware smart response drafting and automated deliverable extraction."

---

### 2:15 - 3:00 | 4. AI Hybrid Search
- **Speaker**: "Natural language hybrid search indexes every message, page, and notification across all 6 integrations."
- **Action**: Type into search bar: `"What did my professor say about the project?"`.
- **Key Takeaway**: "Instant semantic cross-platform discovery."

---

### 3:00 - 4:00 | 5. Live Agent Execution Timeline & Health Telemetry
- **Speaker**: "Underneath the hood, Converra One runs a 9-agent sequential pipeline connected via MCP tools and resources."
- **Action**: Switch to **Agent Timeline Widget** (`resource://agent/timeline`) and **Agent Health Widget** (`resource://agent/health`).
- **Key Takeaway**: "Total transparency into AI agent execution traces, order, latency, and system health."

---

### 4:00 - 5:00 | 6. Enterprise Integrations & Conclusion
- **Speaker**: "With 6 platform adapters, OAuth rate limiting, and 100% NitroStack Cloud readiness, Converra One is where conversations converge."
- **Action**: Show Platform Status widget displaying 6 connected platforms.
- **Key Takeaway**: "Production-ready Agentic AI Communication Platform."
