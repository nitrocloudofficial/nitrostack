# Invenio — Vision

## Mission

Invenio helps researchers discover relationships that traditional literature search cannot.

Instead of only searching papers, Invenio connects:

- **Scientific papers** (OpenAlex, ArXiv, Semantic Scholar, PubMed)
- **Research datasets** (Kaggle, HuggingFace, Zenodo, UCI ML Repository)
- **Code repositories** (GitHub, GitLab, Papers with Code)
- **Patents** (USPTO, EPO, Google Patents)
- **Biological phenomena** (cross-domain biological inspiration)
- **Natural phenomena** (physics, chemistry, ecology)
- **Mathematical concepts** (theorems, proofs, structures)
- **Algorithms** (computational methods and implementations)
- **Engineering techniques** (applied methods and processes)

To uncover meaningful research opportunities that emerge at the intersections of these domains.

---

## The Problem

Modern research is siloed. A biologist studying protein folding may not know that a computer scientist solved an analogous optimization problem. A materials engineer may not find the fluid dynamics paper that explains why their process keeps failing. A neuroscientist may miss the reinforcement learning paper that perfectly models the behavior they're studying.

Traditional literature search only connects you to what you explicitly search for. It doesn't tell you what you *should* be searching for.

---

## The Solution

Invenio is built on a single core insight: **knowledge is a graph, not a library.**

By building a knowledge graph that spans disciplines, Invenio can:

1. **Surface non-obvious connections** — "This algorithm from your field is isomorphic to this technique in another"
2. **Generate cross-domain hypotheses** — "This dataset + this method from a different field could solve your problem"
3. **Map the evidence landscape** — "Here's every paper that supports or contradicts this claim"
4. **Identify research gaps** — "Nobody has connected these two well-established findings"

---

## Design Philosophy

### Operating System, Not Chatbot

Invenio is designed to feel like an operating system for scientific discovery. Like a developer's IDE, it should:
- Be always open, always useful
- Surface information proactively, not just reactively
- Organize your work, not just answer questions
- Get faster and smarter the more you use it

### Cross-Domain Discovery

The core value proposition is **cross-domain insight**. Invenio deliberately indexes heterogeneous entity types (papers, datasets, repos, patents, phenomena, concepts) to create an unprecedented knowledge graph density.

### Evidence-First

Every claim Invenio surfaces is traceable to source entities. Evidence chains are explicit, scored, and inspectable.

### Developer-Grade UX

The interface is modeled after tools researchers already love:
- **Linear** — Clean, fast, keyboard-driven
- **Vercel** — Professional, information-dense
- **Cursor** — Context-aware, command-palette-centric
- **Notion** — Flexible, document-oriented
- **Perplexity** — Source-cited, synthesis-focused
