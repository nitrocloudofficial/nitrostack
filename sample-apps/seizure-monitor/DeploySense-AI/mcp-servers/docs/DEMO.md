# DeploySense AI Demo Guide

To demonstrate the autonomous capabilities:

1. Open NitroStack Studio.
2. Load the `deploysense-ai` server.
3. Open the **MCP Tools** panel.
4. Invoke `investigateIncident` from the Investigation Module.
5. Observe the AI automatically gathering evidence from GitHub, Logs, Kubernetes, and Metrics.
6. Check the mocked Slack and Jira outputs.
7. Open the Widget viewer to see the `Incident Dashboard` and `Investigation Timeline` update visually.
