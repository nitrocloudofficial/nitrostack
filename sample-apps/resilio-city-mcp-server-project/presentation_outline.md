# Hackathon Pitch Outline: City Resilience Network Simulator

## The Hook (Slide 1)
*"Cities are flying blind. They wait for infrastructure to collapse, then spend billions fixing it. We built a proactive immunization engine."*

## The Problem (Slide 2 & 3)
- Climate stress is increasing.
- Infrastructure (Power & Roads) is heavily interdependent.
- We cannot afford to harden everything. How do we choose what to save?

## The Math (Slide 4)
- **Visual:** Show the raw network topology (Barabási–Albert for power, Grid for roads).
- **Talking Point:** "We mathematically model the city as an interdependent graph. When one node fails, its load redistributes to neighbors. If they exceed capacity, they fail too—creating a cascading blackout."

## The AI Prescriptive Engine (Slide 5)
- **Visual:** Show the simulation dashboard with the optimization algorithm running.
- **Talking Point:** "We don't just simulate failure; we prescribe the solution. Our engine calculates *Betweenness Centrality* to identify the most critical bottlenecks, then cross-references it with upgrade costs to calculate a **Resilience ROI**."

## The Financial Impact (Slide 6)
- **Visual:** The final surviving network after targeted upgrades.
- **Talking Point:** "By strategically hardening just 5 critical nodes for $1M, we prevent 40% of the network from collapsing. This is proactive immunization saving hundreds of millions in disaster recovery."

## The Demo (Live or Video)
- Show the baseline failure at 10% stress (nodes turn red).
- Set budget to $2,000,000.
- Run Post-Action Simulation.
- Show the targeted upgrades (green) saving the surrounding grid.
