# PRESENTATION.md

**Purpose:** Plan the final 3-minute demo and backup presentation flow for ClinicaMind.

## Demo Outline

1. **Introduction (30s)**
   - "ClinicaMind is our AI clinical assistant that listens to doctor-patient consultations and orchestrates clinical data into evidence-backed recommendations."
2. **Scenario Walkthrough (2m)**
   - Use the Complex Case: patient says chest pain and cough.
   - Show how nodes appear in the canvas as the system invokes History, Medication, Research, and Gap Analysis.
   - Highlight the pneumonia risk warning and allergy alert.
3. **Quick Glimpse (30s)**
   - Show the interaction warning case or mild cold case briefly.
4. **Conclusion (30s)**
   - Summarize the difference: multi-agent orchestration, live canvas, and clinician-centered design.
   - Mention future work: EHR integration, HIPAA-ready security.

## Slides Outline

- Slide 1: Title, team, project name.
- Slide 2: Problem and vision.
- Slide 3: Architecture diagram from `ARCHITECTURE.md`.
- Slide 4: Demo screenshots or live UI.
- Slide 5: Team and next steps.

## Backup Plan

- Record a short video of the live demo.
- Have a fallback script ready if the live app fails.

## Key Points

- Multi-agent orchestration rather than a single chatbot.
- Real-time canvas that visualizes agent outputs.
- Prototype with mock data but production-ready architecture.
