# Legal & Compliance Policy — TechNova Solutions

**Policy ID:** POL-LEG-01
**Owner:** Legal & Compliance Team
**Last Reviewed:** January 2026
**Applies To:** All product, engineering, and business decisions affecting customers or regulated data

## 1. Purpose
This policy ensures TechNova's products, features, and business practices
remain compliant with applicable laws and regulations across all regions of
operation (India, United States, European Union).

## 2. Regulatory Compliance
2.1. All products and features must comply with GDPR for EU customers, the
     DPDP Act 2023 for Indian customers, and CCPA/CPRA for California
     customers.
2.2. Any feature involving healthcare customer data must comply with HIPAA
     (for US healthcare clients) and undergo additional compliance review.
2.3. Any feature involving financial transaction data must comply with
     relevant financial regulations (e.g., PCI-DSS for payment data).

## 3. AI & Algorithmic Accountability
3.1. Any AI/ML feature that makes or influences decisions affecting users
     (recommendations, risk scoring, personalization, automated
     approvals/rejections) must be reviewed for algorithmic bias before
     launch.
3.2. If an AI feature materially affects a user outcome, the company must be
     able to provide a reasonable explanation of the decision logic upon
     user request.
3.3. AI features must not use protected characteristics (race, religion,
     gender, disability status, etc.) as direct or proxy inputs without
     explicit legal review and justification.

## 4. Vendor & Contract Requirements
4.1. Contracts with third-party vendors must include liability clauses
     covering data breaches, service outages, and indemnification terms.
4.2. Vendor contracts involving personal data must include a Data
     Processing Agreement (DPA) as an addendum.
4.3. Legal must review and approve all vendor contracts before signature.

## 5. Regulated Data Handling
5.1. Any feature or system change involving healthcare or financial customer
     data must undergo additional compliance review beyond the standard
     process, including a documented risk assessment.
5.2. Cross-border transfers of regulated data require additional legal
     review of the destination jurisdiction's data protection adequacy.

## 6. Public Communications
6.1. Legal must be consulted before any public announcement, marketing
     claim, or press release related to a new feature that processes
     personal data or makes claims about security/privacy.

## 7. Non-Compliance Handling
7.1. Any non-compliance finding identified during review must be resolved
     within 30 days of identification.
7.2. Findings that cannot be resolved within 30 days must be escalated to
     the executive team with a documented remediation timeline.

## 8. Audit Trail
8.1. All compliance reviews, sign-offs, and decisions must be documented and
     retained for a minimum of 3 years for audit purposes.
