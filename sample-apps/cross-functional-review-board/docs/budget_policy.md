# Budget & Vendor Approval Policy — TechNova Solutions

**Policy ID:** POL-BUD-01
**Owner:** Finance & Operations Team
**Last Reviewed:** March 2026
**Applies To:** All departments requesting new tools, vendors, or budget allocations

## 1. Purpose
This policy governs how new vendor contracts, tools, and budget requests are
evaluated and approved at TechNova, to ensure responsible spending as the
company manages runway ahead of a planned Series D raise.

## 2. Approval Thresholds
2.1. Any new vendor contract exceeding **$10,000/year** requires sign-off
     from both Finance and Legal before signing.
2.2. Contracts exceeding **$50,000/year** additionally require sign-off from
     the relevant department VP and the CFO.
2.3. Contracts exceeding **$100,000/year** require executive team approval.

## 3. Recurring Costs
3.1. All recurring SaaS subscriptions (cloud providers, third-party AI
     tools, analytics platforms, etc.) must be reviewed annually for
     cost-effectiveness, utilization, and continued necessity.
3.2. Underutilized subscriptions (less than 40% seat/feature utilization)
     flagged during review must be renegotiated or cancelled.

## 4. Security & Data Vendors
4.1. Any new tool or vendor that will have access to customer data must go
     through a Security review in addition to standard Finance approval —
     **regardless of contract value**.
4.2. Vendors handling regulated data (healthcare, financial) require
     additional compliance review and may require a higher approval tier
     regardless of cost.

## 5. Business Justification Requirements
5.1. Departments must submit a written business justification for any new
     hire, tool, or vendor request that exceeds the department's allocated
     budget by more than 10%.
5.2. Business justifications must include: expected ROI/impact, alternatives
     considered, and a rollback plan if the tool underperforms.

## 6. Emergency Purchases
6.1. Emergency purchases (e.g., critical security patches, incident response
     tools, urgent infrastructure fixes) may bypass standard approval
     workflows but must be reported to Finance within 48 hours with
     retroactive justification.

## 7. Vendor Offboarding
7.1. When a vendor contract ends or is cancelled, the requesting department
     must confirm data deletion/return from the vendor within 30 days and
     document this confirmation for audit purposes.

## 8. Enforcement
Purchases made without following this policy may be reversed, and repeated
violations will be escalated to department leadership and Finance.
