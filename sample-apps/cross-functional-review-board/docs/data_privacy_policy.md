# Data Privacy Policy — TechNova Solutions

**Policy ID:** POL-PRIV-01
**Owner:** Legal & Compliance Team
**Last Reviewed:** January 2026
**Applies To:** All employees, contractors, and third-party vendors handling customer or employee data

## 1. Purpose
This policy governs how TechNova collects, stores, processes, and shares
personal data belonging to customers, end-users, and employees. It exists to
protect customer trust and ensure compliance with GDPR (EU), DPDP Act (India),
and CCPA (California) across all regions TechNova operates in.

## 2. Data Collection Principles
2.1. Customer personal data (name, email, browsing behavior, payment info,
     usage patterns, IP address) may only be collected with explicit,
     informed user consent — pre-checked consent boxes are not acceptable.
2.2. Data collection must follow the principle of data minimization: only
     collect what is strictly necessary for the stated purpose.
2.3. Any feature that uses customer behavioral data (e.g., browsing history,
     click patterns, usage frequency) for personalization, scoring, or AI-driven
     recommendations MUST be reviewed by both Legal and Security before launch.

## 3. Third-Party Data Sharing
3.1. Customer data must never be shared with a third-party vendor unless that
     vendor has signed a Data Processing Agreement (DPA).
3.2. Vendors processing personal data must demonstrate adequate security
     controls (SOC 2 Type II report or equivalent) before onboarding.
3.3. Cross-border data transfers (e.g., EU customer data processed outside
     the EU) require a documented legal transfer mechanism (Standard
     Contractual Clauses or equivalent).

## 4. Data Retention & Deletion
4.1. Customer data may not be retained for longer than 24 months without an
     active, documented business justification.
4.2. Users must be able to request deletion of their personal data
     ("right to be forgotten"), and such requests must be honored within 30 days.
4.3. Data used to train AI/ML models must be anonymized or pseudonymized
     wherever technically feasible.

## 5. AI & Automated Decision-Making
5.1. Any AI feature that materially affects a user (recommendations that
     influence purchasing decisions, automated scoring, etc.) must have
     documented logic that can be explained to an affected user upon request.
5.2. AI models trained on user data must be evaluated for bias before
     deployment to production.

## 6. Breach Notification
6.1. Any suspected data breach must be reported internally to the Security
     and Legal teams within 24 hours of discovery.
6.2. Affected users and relevant regulators must be notified within 72 hours,
     in line with GDPR Article 33/34 requirements.

## 7. Required Reviews Before Launch
Every new feature or product change touching personal data must complete a
**Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA)** before implementation. The PIA must be
signed off by Legal and Security.

## 8. Enforcement
Violations of this policy may result in project rollback, mandatory
remediation, and — in serious cases — escalation to the executive team and
disciplinary action.
