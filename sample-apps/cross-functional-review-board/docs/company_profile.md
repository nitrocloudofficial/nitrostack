# Company Profile

## TechNova Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

**Founded:** 2016
**Headquarters:** Bengaluru, India (with offices in Austin, USA and Berlin, Germany)
**Industry:** Enterprise SaaS — Productivity, Collaboration & Workflow Automation
**Employees:** 450 (across Product, Engineering, Security, Legal, Sales, Support, and Operations)
**Funding Stage:** Series C ($62M raised to date)
**Annual Revenue:** ~$28M ARR
**Customer Base:** 1,200+ enterprise clients across Finance, Healthcare, Retail, and EdTech sectors, spanning India, US, and EU regions

### Company Background
TechNova Solutions started in 2016 as a 5-person startup building a simple
task-management tool for small teams. Over the years it evolved into a full
enterprise productivity suite offering project management, internal
communication, document collaboration, and workflow automation tools used by
mid-size and large enterprises. Because TechNova's customers include
healthcare providers and financial institutions, the company handles a mix of
regular business data and highly sensitive regulated data (patient records,
financial transactions), which means compliance and security carry
significant weight in every major decision.

### Company Values (referenced by agents during review)
1. **Customer trust first** — no feature ships if it compromises customer data trust.
2. **Move fast, but validate** — speed matters, but not at the cost of skipping review for regulated data or high-risk changes.
3. **Compliance is non-negotiable** — especially given healthcare and financial clients.
4. **Cost-consciousness** — as a Series C company, TechNova is mindful of burn rate and vendor spend.

### Current Strategic Priorities (2026)
- Expanding AI-powered features across the product suite to stay competitive.
- Reducing infrastructure costs ahead of a planned Series D raise.
- Strengthening compliance posture to unlock more healthcare and BFSI (Banking,
  Financial Services, Insurance) enterprise deals, which require SOC 2 Type II
  and HIPAA-readiness.
- Improving internal tooling and employee productivity without compromising
  security posture.

### Why This Company Works Well for the Demo
Because TechNova sits at the intersection of AI features, regulated customer
data, cost pressure, and compliance requirements, almost any realistic
proposal (new AI feature, vendor adoption, infra migration, access policy
change) naturally creates genuine tension between departments — which is
exactly what the Cross-Functional Decision Review Board is built to surface.
