# Meeting 2 Mission (Haul) — Hackathon Presentation Pitch

A high-impact, 2-to-3 minute presentation script for pitching your project at the hackathon.

---

## ⏱️ Timeline Breakdown
* **0:00 - 0:45:** The Hook & The Problem
* **0:45 - 1:45:** The 4 Agents (Live Demo / Explanation)
* **1:45 - 2:15:** The Tech Stack & Architecture
* **2:15 - 2:30:** Conclusion & Closing Tagline

---

## 🎤 Presentation Script

### **1. The Hook (0:00 - 0:45)**
> "Good morning/afternoon everyone. During hackathons and fast-paced team projects, we spend hours in meetings planning what to do. But here’s the problem: **meetings are where action items go to die.** We walk out with great ideas, but translating them into actual tasks, calendar dates, and risk mitigations is manual, slow, and easy to forget.
>
> That is why we built **Meeting 2 Mission**, powered by **Haul**. It’s not just a meeting transcriber; it’s an AI Command Center that listens to meeting audio, extracts intent, and immediately triggers **4 distinct project management agents** to execute the work for you."

### **2. The 4 Agents (0:45 - 1:45)**
> "Here is how the command center works. When you speak to the assistant during a call, it parses your intent and automatically routes it to one of our four specialized agents:
>
> 1. **The Task Agent:** If you say *'Assign UI design to the designer by Wednesday,'* it instantly parses the owner, the title, and the deadline, writing it straight to the project database.
> 2. **The Calendar Agent:** If you schedule a demo or review call, it hooks into the Google Calendar API, books the slot, generates a meeting link, and schedules it automatically.
> 3. **The Risk Agent:** This agent proactively listens for blockers. If a developer mentions *'Authentication is delayed by 3 days,'* the Risk Agent flags it as High Risk, calculates the downstream impact on launch, and suggests assigning a pair programmer.
> 4. **The Progress Agent:** It actively monitors task statuses, checks if current developer or designer items are overdue, and directly suggests workload reassignments to keep the sprint on track."

### **3. The Tech Stack & Architecture (1:45 - 2:15)**
> "On the frontend, we built a premium, glassmorphic dark-theme dashboard using **Next.js** with real-time polling to reflect database updates. On the backend, we utilized **NitroStack MCP** (Model Context Protocol), registering nine specific tools and custom system prompts. By using MCP, we decoupled the agent logic from the interface, allowing you to use this command center directly from tools like NitroStudio, Cursor, or any custom client."

### **4. Conclusion (2:15 - 2:30)**
> "Meeting 2 Mission bridges the gap between talking about work and actually executing it. No more post-meeting admin. Just speak, align, and watch the agents build your roadmap.
>
> Thank you.
> 
> **— Haul makes life easier 🚀**"
